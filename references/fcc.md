# FCC

FCC ID `OEJGLASWIFI`, applicant Johnson Controls Inc, product code `GLASWIFI`. Wireless thermostat, 2402-2480 MHz.

The grant includes internal photos, external photos, the label, and the manual. The internal photos are the public picture of the board, which is the practical place to look for test pads before opening a unit. No UART pinout has been copied out of them into this repo.

https://fccid.io/OEJGLASWIFI/Internal-Photos/Internal-photo-3658910

The residential unit in the captures was on 2.4 GHz, a 20 MHz channel, channel 6. That fits the grant. It was not seen on 5 GHz. The first radio never got a clean 5 GHz listen, and once the thermostat was found on channel 6 there was no need to keep chasing it.
